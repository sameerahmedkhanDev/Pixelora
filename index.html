FAQ

Questions I hear most

What do I need to start?

Just your idea. Tell me what the app should do and who will use it. Examples of apps you like are helpful, but not required.

Do you build both websites and mobile apps?

Yes. I build web apps and websites, and mobile apps for iOS and Android with React Native, including the backend and database behind them.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the features. A small portal is much quicker than a full booking platform. After we talk, I give you a clear timeline before any work begins.

Can I order through Fiverr?

Yes. My Fiverr gigs are listed in the pricing section. You can also message me here for larger projects.

Will I see progress while you build?

Yes. I share updates as the project moves forward, so you can give feedback early and nothing surprises you at the end.

Do you help after launch?

Yes. I stay available for fixes and improvements after your project goes live.
